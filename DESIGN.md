# Carbon Calculator — Carbon Tally

## Visual world

A night ledger under a green desk lamp: every activity is a line, the category breakdown is a set of measured marks, and the total stays provisional rather than pretending to be a certified number.

## System

- Palette: carbon black `#101311`, soft black `#171d18`, paper `#f0eee4`, green `#b5ce75`, orange `#e18c55`, blue `#91c1bf`.
- Type: Georgia makes the question human; Courier New makes factors and units inspectable.
- Composition: total thesis → activity ledger → category marks → add-one-line form.
- Signature interaction: adding and removing activities recalculates the total and all three category bars immediately.
- Responsive rule: the ledger remains the first read; the breakdown and form become vertical, not hidden.

## Integrity

Emission factors are synthetic defaults and the page explicitly says estimate, no offset, no audit, and no certification.
