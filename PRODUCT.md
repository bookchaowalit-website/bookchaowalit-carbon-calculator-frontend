# Carbon Calculator — Product Truth

Carbon Calculator is a lightweight personal activity ledger that estimates a demonstrative footprint from travel, energy, and food inputs. It is an educational estimate, not an audited emissions report.

## User and job

- Audience: a curious individual deciding which everyday activity to examine first.
- Primary job: enter a few activities, see the estimate by category, and understand the assumptions behind the result.
- Mode: operate.

## Boundaries

- Emission factors are clearly labelled synthetic defaults.
- No offset purchase, regulatory reporting, supplier data, or scientific certification is claimed.
- The first release is client-side and keeps the entered ledger in the current browser only.
